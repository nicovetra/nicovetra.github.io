The Stories We Tell Ourselves

Poem · July 10, 2026
Life is what we make it. We tell stories to feed the imagination. The stories we tell ourselves can drive us to crazy realizations. We lie to ourselves. If you don’t believe in yourself first, then why should anyone else believe in you. Vivid ideas coming to fruition. So lively the stories are palpable. They follow us all day long, and bug us when we least expect. The stories we tell ourselves take over our brains. Infatuating us with new loves. Filling our minds with passionate obsessions. The stories we tell ourselves are powerful tools. Systems which can push us to new heights. Or which bring our towers collapsing down. Our stories show up in our dreams, fueling our desires. But our stories can also torment us. Driving us mad. Paralyzing our thoughts. Tossing and turning at the reality that we keep telling ourselves must exist, but which fails to materialize. Our stories are how we live. Without them, life becomes a series of collisions we have no control over. Without our stories, we lose meaning. We lose purpose and clarity. Our stories lie to us…. But they keep us sane. Our stories can build empires, and our stories can strike down dynasties. The story we tell ourselves is the most powerful of all. What we choose to believe about ourselves directs our lives. No leap is too big if we just tell ourselves to start small. No stretch of the imagination is too far if we materialize it. Our imagination, the stories we tell ourselves, are fuel for the fire of what life can be. And without belief in our own story, we can’t even begin moving down the proverbial path of life. Without imagination, there is only entropy.
